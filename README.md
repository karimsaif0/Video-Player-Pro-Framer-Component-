## Keyboard Shortcuts

When the player is focused:

- **Space** — Play / pause
- **M** — Mute / unmute
- **F** — Enter / exit fullscreen
- **Left Arrow** — Seek backward 5 seconds
- **Right Arrow** — Seek forward 5 seconds

## Framer Controls

### Video
Upload the video file used by the player.

### Poster
Add an optional image displayed before playback begins.

### Autoplay
Automatically starts playback when the player loads. Browser autoplay policies may require muted playback.

### Muted
Starts the video without sound.

### Loop
Automatically restarts the video when it reaches the end.

### Hover Controls
Shows the custom controls when the pointer enters the player and hides them when it leaves.

### Hover Fade
Controls the transition speed of the hover controls in milliseconds.

### Controls
Shows or hides the complete custom control panel.

### Progress
Shows the progress and seek bar.

### Time
Shows the current playback time and total duration.

### Volume
Shows the mute button and volume slider.

### Speed
Shows the playback-speed selector.

### Fullscreen
Shows the fullscreen control.

### Speeds
Enter comma-separated playback speeds from 0.25x to 4x.

Example:

`0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2`

### Default Speed
Sets the initial playback speed.

### Video Radius
Controls the corner radius of the video.

### Controls Radius
Controls the corner radius of the control panel.

### Controls Opacity
Controls the transparency of the control-panel background.

### Controls Blur
Controls the backdrop blur behind the controls.

### Control Color
Sets the color of the player controls.

### Accent Color
Sets the color used by progress and volume indicators.

### Progress Height
Controls the thickness of the progress bar.

### Control Padding
Controls the internal spacing around the control panel.

### Control Gap
Controls spacing between control groups.

### Object Fit
Choose how the video fits inside the player:

- Cover
- Contain
- Fill

### Background
Sets the background color behind the video.

## Design Approach

Video Player Pro is designed to give you a clean player without forcing a specific visual style. The component exposes the controls that matter most for a polished Framer build while keeping the interface compact and responsive.

Use the Framer property controls to match the player to your brand, from the control colors and accent color to the radius, blur, opacity, spacing, and playback behavior.

## Browser Behavior

Autoplay is subject to browser autoplay policies. If a browser blocks autoplay with sound, enabling **Muted** generally provides the most reliable autoplay behavior.

Fullscreen availability can vary by browser and device.

## Installation

1. Open the component in Framer.
2. Add the Video Player Pro component to your canvas.
3. Upload your video through the **Video** property.
4. Optionally add a **Poster** image.
5. Configure playback, controls, appearance, and sizing from the property panel.
6. Publish your Framer site.
